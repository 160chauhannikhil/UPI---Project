## 🔑 Key Insights

- **10,000× scale** — UPI grew from 0.01 TPS (2016) to 5,400 TPS (2024)
- **Zero MDR (Jan 2020)** — Banks lost ₹2,000 Cr/year revenue; adoption tripled
- **Layered architecture** — Each stakeholder got sovereignty over their own layer, ending turf wars
- **23% of failures** originate at PSU bank CBS layer — not NPCI's switch
- **Current bottleneck** — CBS modernisation at public sector banks
- **Next frontier** — Cross-border UPI (7 countries live), Credit on UPI, CBDC integration
